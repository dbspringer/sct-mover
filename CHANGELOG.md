# Changelog

## 1

First release, for WoW: Forever (the 1.60 client).

- Lift or lower the text above your target with the "Target height" slider, so it clears the nameplate. It only changes two game settings, so your choice stays even if you turn the addon off
- Draw the text near your character above other UI elements with one checkbox
- Move the text near your character anywhere on the screen: drag a box in Move Mode (with sample text to show the result), or set exact values with the "Horizontal" and "Vertical" sliders
- Share self text settings across all your characters, or give one character its own with a checkbox
- Open the options with `/sctm` or `/sctmover`, and start dragging with `/sctm move`
- Show the version and locale at the bottom of the options, for bug reports
- Add machine translations for German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Korean, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese
